Referral Guidelines

What Makes a Valid Referral

The rules below explain how the referral is tracked and when a commission is earned.

New Client Only

The client must not already be in BuilderK's pipeline. If they've had prior contact with us, the referral won't be valid.

Submit Before First Consultation

Your referral must be submitted before your client has their first consultation with BuilderK. First come, first served.

First Referrer Wins

If the same client is submitted by more than one person, the first verified submission gets credit, no exceptions.

Valid for 12 Months

Your referral stays active for 12 months from submission date. If the client signs a contract within that window, you're paid.

FAQ

Common Questions

Everything you need to know before submitting your first referral.

How long is my referral valid?
12 months from the date you submit it. If your client signs a construction contract with BuilderK within that window, you earn the commission.
Can I refer multiple clients?
Absolutely. There's no cap on how many clients you can refer. Each referral is tracked separately and each one qualifies for its own 1.5% commission.
Do I need to be a licensed real estate agent to participate?
No. Anyone can refer a client and earn the commission: real estate agents, mortgage brokers, friends, family, or anyone else who connects us with a client who builds with BuilderK.
Can I be the buyer's agent AND earn the referral fee?
Yes. Your normal real estate commission on the lot purchase is a separate transaction from the 1.5% BuilderK referral fee. Both are yours, they don't offset each other in any way.
How do I track my referral status?
We'll keep you updated by email at each milestone. You can also reach out anytime at info@builderk.com or (239) 230 4868.
When exactly do I get paid?
Your first payment (0.75%) is issued when the client's construction loan is approved by the lender. Your second payment (0.75%) is issued at certificate of occupancy, the moment the build is complete and ready for the client to move in.
