Why Cape Coral

Why Cape Coral Is the Place to Build

Cape Coral offers canal living and Gulf access. Seawalls, flood zones, utilities, elevation, and coastal requirements should be reviewed for each lot before design and pricing.

Canal and Waterfront Living

Cape Coral includes Gulf access canals, freshwater canals, and channels with different bridge and depth restrictions. The waterway, seawall, and access conditions should be confirmed before design.

Southwest Florida Growth

Cape Coral is one of the fastest growing cities in the United States. Property values continue to climb, especially on canal front lots. Building new construction here means designing a home for the lot and current code while recognizing that future value depends on the market and the completed appraisal.

Build on Your Lot

Own a lot in Lee County? Its appraised equity may reduce or replace cash required by a lender, depending on the appraisal, total project cost, borrower qualifications, and loan program. We build on canal and dry lots across Cape Coral and Southwest Florida.

Common Questions

Building a Custom Home in Cape Coral?

Everything you need to know before building in Southwest Florida's waterfront capital.

How much does it cost to build a custom home in Cape Coral?
BuilderK's shared calculator uses Central Florida base planning rates of Standard ($160 to $180/sqft), Midrange ($180 to $220/sqft), and Luxury ($220 to $280+/sqft), last updated July 18, 2026. It returns one combined planning range from the selections entered. Land, financing, government permit and impact fees, utilities, unusual site work, and the calculator's other listed exclusions are not included. Cape Coral flood zone, canal front, seawall, utility, and site requirements can materially change the range. After we review the plan, scope and site, we send a formal proposal. Use the planning calculator for an early range.
How long does it take to build a custom home in Cape Coral?
A typical custom home in Cape Coral takes 6 to 10 months from permit approval to move in. The full timeline including design, financing, and Lee County permitting is usually 9 to 14 months. Waterfront builds may require additional time for seawall inspection and flood zone compliance. BuilderK provides regular progress updates throughout the entire build.
Can I build on my own lot in Lee County?
Yes. If you own a lot in Cape Coral, Lee County, or anywhere in Southwest Florida, BuilderK can build your custom home on it. Your lot's appraised equity may reduce or replace cash required by a lender, depending on the appraisal, total project cost, borrower qualifications, and loan program. We build on canal lots, Gulf access lots, freshwater lots, and dry lots. Learn about financing
What permits do I need to build a home in Cape Coral?
All new home construction in Cape Coral requires building permits from both Lee County and the City of Cape Coral. This includes site plans, engineering, flood zone compliance, wind load calculations, and environmental reviews. Canal front lots may need additional seawall and dock permits. BuilderK handles the entire permitting process so you don't have to navigate it alone.
What credit score do I need for a construction loan?
Construction loan requirements vary by lender and program. Many programs look for a credit score around 680, while others accept lower scores or are based on assets and weigh the project more than the borrower's credit. BuilderK connects you with lenders who specialize in construction to permanent loans, and we handle all the builder paperwork to streamline your approval. Check your eligibility
