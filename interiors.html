Your Home, Your Choices

Interior Selections

Every finish in your BuilderK home is chosen by you. From cabinets to countertops to the paint on the walls, we walk you through every decision so your home feels like yours from day one.

View Our Interior Gallery

Fully Customizable at Every Build Level

Unlike other builders who lock you into curated packages, BuilderK lets you choose your own cabinets, countertops, flooring, fixtures, lighting, and paint at every build level. Your structure defines the tier. Your interior is always your call.

Photos show examples of each option.

Kitchen Cabinets

The kitchen is the center of your home. We offer a full range of cabinet styles, door profiles, materials, and hardware so you can create the exact look you want, from clean modern flat panels to timeless shaker designs.

Shaker Style

The most popular door style in new construction. Clean recessed panels that work with any direction, farmhouse, transitional, or modern.

  • Timeless
  • Versatile
  • Easy to clean

Flat Panel (Slab)

Smooth, frameless doors for a sleek minimalist look. A favorite for modern and contemporary kitchens. No grooves, no detail, just clean surfaces.

  • Modern
  • Minimalist
  • Sleek

Raised Panel

A center panel raised above the frame for a classic, elegant feel. Perfect for traditional and Mediterranean influenced Florida homes.

  • Classic elegance
  • Detailed
  • Traditional

Glass Front Inserts

Mix solid and glass front doors to display dishes or add depth. Available in clear, frosted, seeded, or textured glass.

  • Visual depth
  • Display storage
  • Custom

Thermofoil (MDF Core)

A vinyl layer bonded to MDF. Resistant to moisture and easy to maintain, ideal for Florida's humidity. Comes in dozens of colors and finishes.

  • Moisture resistant
  • Low maintenance
  • Many colors

Solid Hardwood

Real maple, birch, or oak cabinets with natural grain and lasting durability. Can be stained, painted, or left natural. The premium choice.

  • Natural beauty
  • Durable
  • Refinishable

Soft Close Everything

Every cabinet comes with soft close hinges and full extension drawers as standard. No slamming, full access to the back of every drawer.

  • Quiet close
  • Full access
  • Standard

Countertops

Your countertops set the tone for kitchens and bathrooms. We offer everything from engineered quartz to natural stone, each with different durability, maintenance, and aesthetic profiles perfectly suited for Florida living.

Quartz (Engineered Stone)

Nonporous, resistant to stains, and easy to maintain. Consistent patterns throughout the slab. Handles Florida's humidity perfectly, no sealing required, ever.

  • Nonporous
  • Stain resistant
  • No sealing
  • Humidity proof

Granite (Natural Stone)

Every slab is unique with natural veining and depth. Extremely resistant to heat, set a hot pan right on it. Hundreds of colors from subtle to dramatic. Needs annual sealing.

  • Made for You
  • Heat resistant
  • Natural beauty

Quartzite (Natural)

Looks like marble but performs like granite. Incredible veining with the hardness to handle daily kitchen use. The choice for those who want natural beauty without the softness of marble.

  • Marble look
  • Granite strength
  • Stunning veining

Marble

Timeless elegance with dramatic veining. Softer than granite, it develops a beautiful patina over time. Best for bathrooms, feature islands, and lovers of comfortable luxury.

  • Timeless
  • Dramatic veining
  • Luxury feel

Flooring

Florida homes need flooring that handles humidity, heavy foot traffic, and still looks amazing years later. We help you pick the right material for every room, from living areas to wet spaces.

Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP)

The #1 choice for Florida new construction. 100% waterproof, resistant to scratches, and available in incredibly realistic wood and stone looks. Comfortable underfoot and easy to maintain.

  • 100% waterproof
  • Scratch resistant
  • Realistic textures
  • Florida #1

Porcelain Tile

Large Format porcelain (24x24 or 12x48 plank) is the classic Florida flooring. Extremely durable, water resistant, available in wood look, marble look, and concrete finishes.

  • Extremely durable
  • Water resistant
  • Large formats

Ceramic Tile

Versatile and reliable for bathrooms, laundry, and wet areas. Endless patterns, colors, and sizes including subway, hexagon, and decorative mosaics.

  • Versatile
  • Wet area ideal
  • Many patterns

Engineered Hardwood

Real wood veneer over a stable plywood core. More resistant to moisture than solid hardwood. Beautiful natural warmth for bedrooms and living areas in climate controlled spaces.

  • Real wood
  • Natural warmth
  • Stable in humidity

Natural Stone (Travertine)

Imported travertine or marble tile for a truly unique floor. Warm tones and Old World character, especially popular in Florida for its Mediterranean feel.

  • Unique character
  • Warm tones
  • Premium

Bathroom Fixtures & Finishes

From the master bath to guest powder rooms, every fixture and finish is selected by you. Vanities, shower systems, tubs, tile, and hardware, we help you create the bathroom you've always wanted.

Walk In Showers

Custom Tile walk in showers with frameless glass enclosures. Choose your tile, niche placement, bench style, and showerhead. From compact to spa inspired.

  • Custom tile
  • Frameless glass
  • Built in niches

Freestanding Tubs

A statement piece for your master bath. Acrylic, cast iron, or stone resin. From classic clawfoot to modern sculptural shapes. We help you pick the right size for your space.

  • Statement piece
  • Multiple styles
  • Spa feel

Vanities & Sinks

Single or double vanities, floating, freestanding, or built in. Pair with vessel sinks, undermount, or integrated basins. Coordinate with your countertop and cabinet choices.

  • Custom configs
  • Multiple styles
  • Coordinated

Hardware Finishes

Chrome, brushed nickel, matte black, brushed gold, or oil rubbed bronze. Every faucet, showerhead, towel bar, and accessory is coordinated to your chosen finish.

  • 5+ finishes
  • Coordinated sets
  • Quality brands

Shower Systems

Standard showerheads, rain showers, handheld combos, or full body jet systems. Thermostatic valves for precise temperature control. Built for daily comfort.

  • Rain showers
  • Body jets
  • Thermostatic

Lighting

Lighting transforms how every room feels. We plan every fixture with you, recessed cans, under cabinet LEDs, statement pendants, and exterior landscape lighting. A complete plan is part of your build.

Recessed LED Cans

The foundation of any lighting plan. Energy Efficient LEDs placed for even, shadowless light. Adjustable color temps from warm (2700K) to daylight (5000K).

  • Energy efficient
  • Even coverage
  • Adjustable warmth

Pendants & Chandeliers

Statement fixtures over kitchen islands, dining tables, and entryways. Hundreds of styles from industrial to coastal to very modern. The pieces people notice first.

  • Statement pieces
  • Hundreds of styles
  • Focal points

Under Cabinet & Task Lighting

LED strips under kitchen cabinets, inside pantries, and in closets. Functional light exactly where you need it for cooking, prepping, and daily life.

  • Functional
  • Hidden install
  • LED strips

Exterior & Landscape

Coach lights at entries, soffit lights, pathway lighting, and palm tree uplighting. Designed for Florida's outdoor living lifestyle and serious curb appeal.

  • Curb appeal
  • Security
  • Outdoor living

Smart Controls & Dimmers

Dimmer switches, smart systems (Lutron Caseta), and scene controls. Set the mood for dinner, movies, or mornings. Control from your phone or voice assistant.

  • App control
  • Scene presets
  • Voice compatible

Paint & Wall Finishes

Color sets the mood of every room. Beyond paint, we offer accent wall treatments, shiplap, board & batten, wainscoting, and stone, to give your home personality and architectural texture.

Interior Paint (Sherwin Williams)

Choose any color from the full Sherwin Williams palette. Eggshell finish on walls for a soft, washable surface. Semi gloss on trim, doors, and wet areas for durability.

  • Any color
  • Washable eggshell
  • Semi gloss trim

Shiplap Accent Walls

Horizontal or vertical boards on a feature wall. Creates texture and depth that flat paint can't achieve. Paintable in any color. Popular in living rooms, bedrooms, and offices.

  • Texture & depth
  • Feature wall
  • Paintable

Board & Batten

Vertical battens applied to walls or at wainscoting height. Adds architectural detail to hallways, dining rooms, and bedrooms. Classic, elegant, and makes ceilings feel taller.

  • Architectural
  • Visual height
  • Elegant

Crown Molding & Trim

Crown molding, chair rail, and upgraded baseboards take your home from basic builder finishes to custom finishes. Available in modern clean profiles or ornate traditional styles.

  • Finished feel
  • Custom profiles
  • Elevated look

Coffered Ceilings & Niches

Coffered or tray ceilings add dimension overhead. Recessed wall niches create display areas. These architectural details make your home feel truly designed, not just built.

  • Dimension
  • Architectural
  • Custom feel
