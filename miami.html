Why Miami

Why Miami Is the Place to Build

South Florida's luxury real estate market attracts buyers from around the world, and building custom lets you create a residence as distinctive as the city itself.

Tropical Modern Architecture

All new home construction in Miami Dade County requires building permits with strict wind load compliance. Every exterior product, windows, doors, roofing, and impact rated materials, must carry a Miami Dade Notice of Acceptance (NOA) to certify it meets the county's enhanced hurricane standards. BuilderK handles all permitting, NOA documentation, flood zone compliance, and inspection scheduling so you never have to deal with the bureaucracy.
Construction loan requirements vary by lender and program. Many programs look for a credit score around 680, while others accept lower scores or are based on assets and weigh the project more than the borrower's credit.
