Why the Space Coast

Why the Space Coast Is the Place to Build

Brevard County combines a major aerospace economy with Atlantic coastline and a wide range of established and growing communities.

Booming Tech Economy

The Space Coast is home to major aerospace and defense employers, including SpaceX, Blue Origin, L3Harris, and Northrop Grumman. Those employers continue to attract new residents to the area.

Beachside Living

Brevard County offers Atlantic coastline, surfing, beachside communities, and no state income tax.
