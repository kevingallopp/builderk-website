Questions

Before you call

Do you pull the permit?
Yes. As the licensed Florida general contractor we pull the permit, schedule the inspections and close out the job with the building department.
Can you work while our business stays open?
Yes. We plan the phases around your hours and keep the work areas separated, so your team and customers can keep using the rest of the building.
Do we need plans before we call?
No. If you have plans, we bid from them. If you do not, we walk the space with you and help you put together the design and engineering the permit needs.
Can you send our vendor paperwork?
Yes. We send a certificate of insurance naming your company, our W9 and our Florida general contractor license, CGC #1537163.
Where do you work?
Orlando and Central Florida, from our office on Millenia Boulevard. Recent commercial jobs include Apopka, Altamonte Springs, Clermont, Winter Haven and Leesburg.
