Privacy Policy

How Builders Knowledge Development Inc. collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Effective Date: September 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Builders Knowledge Development Inc., doing business as BuilderK ("we," "us," or "our"), respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website builderk.com, use our services, or communicate with us.

By using our website or services, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information You Provide

When you fill out a contact form, request a quote, or communicate with us, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Where you plan to build (ZIP code or city)
  • Information about your construction project (lot ownership, budget, timeline, home size preferences)

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:

  • IP address and approximate location
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, and navigation paths
  • Referring website or source

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use Google Analytics and Google Ads to understand website traffic and measure advertising effectiveness. These services may use cookies and similar technologies to collect usage data. For visitors in the United States, Google Analytics and Google Ads measurement is on by default; for visitors elsewhere it stays off unless they allow it. You can turn Google measurement off at any time with the Privacy choices link at the bottom of every page, and we remember that choice on your device for up to 12 months. When you arrive from a Google ad, the ad click identifier is kept with your website inquiry unless you turn measurement off. We honor browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals: when your browser sends one, Google measurement stays off and no ad click identifier is kept. Campaign labels and first and last website visit context are retained in tab session storage for up to two hours to help our team understand submitted inquiries. This control covers Google measurement; other providers and browser settings are described in this policy.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide quotes for construction services
  • Communicate with you about your project or our services
  • Send project updates and notifications (with your consent)
  • Improve our website, services, and customer experience
  • Analyze website usage and advertising performance
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with:

  • Lending partners: If you request financing assistance, we may share relevant project details with our lending partners to help facilitate your construction loan application.
  • Service providers: We use outside services for website hosting (Vercel), form processing (Formspree), analytics (Google Analytics), advertising (Google Ads, Meta Ads), and customer relationship management (GoHighLevel). These providers only access information necessary to perform their services.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or governmental regulation.

5. Text Messages

If you opt in to receive SMS/text messages from BuildShare Development Capital Inc. or BuilderK, you agree to receive project updates and notification messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for assistance.

We will not share your phone number or SMS opt in information with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Lead and project inquiry information is typically retained for up to 3 years after your last interaction with us.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Opt out of SMS communications by replying STOP
  • Disable cookies through your browser settings

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@builderk.com or call (239) 230 4868.

9. Outside Links

Our website may contain links to outside websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any outside sites you visit.

10. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 18, we will take steps to delete it.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
